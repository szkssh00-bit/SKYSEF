SCORING SYSTEM / 35 TEAMS

Four Events × 100 Points

Challenge 1 Faster achieved time receives a higher score. 100 × fastest achieved time ÷ team time
Challenge 2 Faster achieved time receives a higher score. 100 × fastest achieved time ÷ team time
Challenge 3 A larger achieved angle receives a higher score. 100 × team angle ÷ highest achieved angle
Custom Design Vote Votes are normalized because the final number of voters may vary. 100 × team votes ÷ highest vote count

The first-place team in each event receives 100 points. “Didn't achieve Goal” receives 0 points. The overall ranking is determined by the sum of four event scores, with a maximum of 400 points.
